## Overview / Descripción General

**Gray Notation** isn’t an app; it’s a **visual notation system for handwritten notes**. It was created to tackle the chaos of traditional note-taking, offering a set of simple symbols and spatial rules that transform messy sheets of paper into hierarchical, easy-to-read notes that are ready for revision.

### Key Features

* **No need for colours:** Works 100% with just a single pen or pencil.
* **Hierarchical reading:** Your eyes go straight to the most important concepts when revising.
* **Solves analogue fragmentation:** Visual tools to connect scattered ideas when the instructor jumps from one topic to another.
* **Flexible framework:** It is a starting point; you can (and should) adapt it to your own subjects and workflow.

### The Problem It Solves

Taking notes in fast-paced or highly theoretical classes usually leads to:

- **Flat notes:** Everything looks the same, making it hard to spot key concepts, definitions, or formulas at a glance.
- **Fragmented ideas:** Topics get cut off when the class jumps to something else and later returns to the original thread.
- **Colour dependency:** Relying on multiple highlighters, rulers, or coloured pens just to make sense of the page.

> [!important]
> **Gray Notation** is currently in **Beta (`v0.9`)**, so new examples and refinements are actively being added.

> [!tip]
> Gray Notation is designed for notes structured in short sentences or paragraphs (**Blocks**), rather than extensive continuous prose.
